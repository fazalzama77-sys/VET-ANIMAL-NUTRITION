Animal Nutrition Studio

VETERINARY CURRICULUM COMPANION
Overview

A comprehensive, interactive curriculum companion for B.V.Sc. & A.H. Second Year Animal Nutrition (VCI MSVE Syllabus, Credit hours 3+1 = 4). Designed for veterinary undergraduate students at ICAR-IVRI and across India, covering 4 theory units (68 topics), 4 practical units (29 topics), ration formulation, feed technology, laboratory proximate analysis, question banks, quizzes, and an offline-first learning analytics dashboard.

Syllabus Scope
  • Unit 1 (Principles & Feed Technology): Nutrients, energy/protein evaluation, silage/hay conservation, anti-nutritional factors, processing, and additives.
  • Unit 2 (Applied Ruminant Nutrition-I): Scientific feeding, digestion/metabolism trials, digestibility calculation, and ruminant feeding standards.
  • Unit 3 (Applied Ruminant Nutrition-II): Nutrient requirements, ration computation for cattle, buffaloes, sheep and goats, bypass nutrients, and NPN feeding.
  • Unit 4 (Applied Non-Ruminant Nutrition): Feeding swine, equines, broilers, layers, dogs, cats, laboratory animals, and captive wild species.
  • Practical Units 1–4: Weende proximate analysis, Van Soest detergent analysis, mineral estimations, DCP/TDN calculations, and diet formulation.
